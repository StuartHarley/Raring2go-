## 2. Your opening positions (from the homework; your decision)

**A · Invest**
- Stake grows with the round, inside your 0–30% limit: **5–10%** at Level 1, **10–20%** at Level 2, **20–30%** at Level 3.
- Suggested package: board observer (not a board seat), pro-rata rights, a separate commercial agreement for the Flow integration.
- **Do not give:** a right of first refusal on any sale, or long exclusivity. Both limit your exit price. If pressed, offer a right of first *notification* only.

**B · Licence**
- Open at about **70% of the guide price per centre** (about £279 of £399 a month). Room to 65–75%.
- **Minimum 100 centres in year one** (about £335,000 a year guaranteed).
- You run the platform and keep the IP.

**C · Channel**
- Open at **20% recurring** margin on subscriptions Fujifilm sells (about £80 of £399 a month), or **15% of first-year value** as a one-off fee (about £718 per centre). Benchmarks: reseller 15–30%, referral 10–25%.

**Headline if asked for a single number:** Scope 2, Level 2, about **£1.45m** (people, security, AI/infrastructure and marketing, plus 15% contingency).

## 3. Decisions only you can make before the meeting

1. **Walk-away points.** For example: the maximum total stake you would give (your limit is 30%), the minimum licence price per centre, whether you would ever grant exclusivity (suggest: no, or only time-limited and category-specific).
2. **Your ARR.** Monthly subscription income today × 12. At about £9.7m post-money for the headline cell, it needs roughly £0.8–1.4m of ARR at typical multiples. If yours is much lower, expect pushback on the valuation or ask for a bigger stake.
3. **Salary assumptions.** Developer about £55k, customer support £32k, trainer £34k, marketing £42k (all before about 25% on-costs). If your plan is different, the amounts move.
4. **Who from your side attends,** and who has authority to agree terms in the room.

## 4. Questions to ask Fujifilm (listen first)

1. Which of the four paths is closest to how you already work with software partners?
2. Who would own this inside Fujifilm: technology, commercial, or strategy?
3. What would a first step look like: a pilot, a small commitment, or a bigger decision?
4. What do you need to see before the next conversation: results, security certification, references?
5. How does the January 2027 Flow API access fit your timetable?
6. Do you have an existing corporate venture or partnership route we should know about?

## 5. If they ask "what are you asking for?"

Suggested line: *"We've set out four ways to work together and what each could unlock. We'd like to understand what suits you before proposing a figure. The scale of commitment can range from a joint pilot up to a strategic investment. What would be realistic on your side?"*

If pressed for a number: give the **range** (£0.5m to £4.3m depending on scope and level) and the headline level, not the equity.

---

## 6. Things to avoid conceding early

- A valuation or stake in the first meeting.
- Exclusivity in the print software category.
- A right of first refusal on any future sale.
- Sharing the licence and channel opening positions in writing.
- Quoting pilot figures you cannot evidence from the platform. If asked for numbers beyond the scorecard, say you will provide them after the meeting.
